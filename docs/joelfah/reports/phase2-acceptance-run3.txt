Resits work as follows:

1. Register for a resit through the ERP system.
2. Pay the resit fee listed in the ERP for each course. The amount is not stated in the manual but can be found in the ERP.
3. Once the fee is paid, your lecturer enters the resit score.
4. For normal resits, the resit score (out of 70) is added to your Continuous Assessment (CA) mark.
5. The final total is capped at 79, resulting in a grade B+ (3.5 points).
