Resit examinations work as follows:

1. Register for a resit through Open Resit Registration in the ERP.
2. The registration lists your resit courses with their respective resit fee and payment status.
3. Pay the resit fee using the channels provided by My Fees document.
4. After paying, your lecturer enters the resit score (out of 70).
5. For a normal resit: add the resit score to your Continuous Assessment (CA) mark.
6. The final total is capped at 79, which corresponds to grade B+ and 3.5 points.
